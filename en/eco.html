SUSTAINABILITY

Regulation is moving.
Packaging has to move first.

Paper hooks are not a trend, they are a compliance response. The EU Single-Use Plastics Directive, the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation and national plastic taxes have turned plastic hangings into a line item your buyer now has to defend.

THE SHORT VERSION

A hook is 1% of the pack and 100% of the argument

When a retailer audits a supplier's packaging, the hook is the easiest component to challenge: it is plastic, it is small, and it goes straight to landfill with the tag. Replacing it is the cheapest visible win available — which is exactly why buyers ask for it first.

  • Removes a plastic component without redesigning the pack
  • Keeps the existing pegboard and hanging workflow
  • Adds a documentable, certifiable material to the file
REGULATORY TIMELINE

What changed, and when

Dates below reflect the EU and UK framework most of our customers sell into. National transposition varies — confirm with your compliance team.

2019

EU Single-Use Plastics Directive

Adopted. Targets the ten most-found beach litter items and sets the direction for packaging restriction across member states.

2021

SUP Directive transposed nationally

Member states implement bans and marking rules. Retailers begin asking suppliers for plastic-free packaging components.

2022

UK Plastic Packaging Tax

£200 per tonne on plastic packaging with less than 30% recycled content. Plastic components become a cost line, not a free default.

2024

EU PPWR enters into force

The Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation sets recyclability and recycled-content requirements for packaging placed on the EU market.

2025

Recyclability performance grades

Packaging must meet design-for-recycling criteria. Mono-material paper components move from nice-to-have to easy pass.

CHAIN OF CUSTODY

From stump to shipping carton in four steps

This is the part your buyer's compliance officer will ask to see. Each step has a document.

STEP 01

Certified forest

Pulp from FSC®-certified, responsibly managed forests. The certificate covers the forest management unit, not just the mill.

STEP 02

Board mill

Kraft and greyboard produced under chain-of-custody control. Mill invoice references the FSC claim code.

STEP 03

Converting

Die-cutting, printing and foil stamping under the same custody system, segregated from non-certified stock.

STEP 04

Delivery

Certificate copy and lot reference travel with the shipment, so the paperwork reaches your buyer with the goods.

OUR COMMITMENTS

Four things we will put in writing

Certificates on request

FSC® chain-of-custody documentation supplied with every order, not only on request.

Soy-based inks

Standard runs use soy-based ink. Water-based varnish available for food-adjacent packaging.

Offcut recycling

Die-cutting waste is baled and returned to the paper stream rather than sent to general waste.

No greenwashing

We do not claim “biodegradable in landfill” or “100% carbon neutral”. The claims we make can be checked.
