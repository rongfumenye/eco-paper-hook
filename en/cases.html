USE CASES

If it hangs on a peg,
it can hang on paper.

Socks are the starting point, not the limit. Underwear, towels, pet apparel, hair accessories, baby goods, beauty tools, gift sets — every soft product that needs to be seen on a fixture can finish its packaging with a paper hook. Change the hole, change the board, change the industry.

MAIN SCENARIO

Socks: the most typical, and the most demanding

Socks are light, high-SKU and restocked constantly. Three requirements dominate: fast loading, correct hole size, and printing that distinguishes size and style. Paper meets all three — and changing a SKU means changing the print, not the tooling.

  • Ankle and crew socks: 1.5 mm is sufficient
  • Sports and multi-packs: 1.8–2.0 mm recommended
  • Gift boxed: foil stamping and custom silhouette

DISPLAY METHODS

Four fixtures, four hole sizes

Photograph your fixture before ordering. One photo beats ten lines of description.

Pegboard

The most common retail fixture. Inserts into a 4–6 mm hole and must hang vertically without twisting.

Square tube rail

Common in apparel. Match the hole to the rail diameter; rails over 25 mm need a wider throat.

Wire grid

Convenience and promo units. The hook must pass through and lock, or it slides along the wire.

End-cap display

Temporary promotions with high pick-up frequency. The Q-hook is fastest to load.

BEFORE YOU ORDER

Confirm these three things first

  • Peg diameter: decides the hole size. A 1 mm error means it will not hang — measure, do not estimate.
  • Unit weight: decides board thickness. Above 300 g, go thicker or use a double hook.
  • Storage conditions: humid warehousing needs a moisture assessment before you commit.

Send us these three numbers and we will come back with a recommended profile and hole size.
