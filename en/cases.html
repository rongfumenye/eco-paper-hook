SCENE 01

Sock Retail Display

Socks are the most typical use of paper hooks: many SKUs, frequent restocking, and a need to tell sizes and styles apart at a glance. Paper hooks and sock display boards keep each pair facing forward, turning the packaging itself into a display position — helping retailers show more designs in limited shelf space while cutting the time spent on replenishment.

Problem it solves：Solves messy displays and slow restocking across many sock designs
Related product types
Paper sock hooks (H01–H08), sock display boards (B01–B06)
How it is used
Thread the paper hook through the hang hole of the sock header card, or fix socks directly onto a display board, then hang on shelf hooks, pegboards or rails. Changing designs only needs a print change, not a new hook.
Suitable customers
Sock manufacturers, sock brands, retail chains, supermarket buyers
Customisation options
Hook structure, board material, printed artwork and brand colours, surface finishing; dimensions and hole size confirmed against your actual shelf and hanging rail.

SCENE 02

Supermarkets & Convenience Stores

Supermarkets and convenience stores have tight shelf space and a fast restocking rhythm — packaging must hang securely, stay neat and read clearly. Standard paper hooks and display boards can be supplied at scale and work with the shelf and peg systems stores already use, keeping goods aligned and facing forward for easy pickup and quick replenishment.

Problem it solves：Solves tight shelf space, inconsistent displays and slow restocking
Related product types
Standard paper hooks (H02, H04), sock display cards (B02, B03)
How it is used
Match the hole size to the store's shelf hooks or pegboard, then hang header cards on the paper hook for a uniform display; display boards group multiple items for end-caps and promotion areas.
Suitable customers
Supermarket buyers, convenience chains, FMCG brands, distributors
Customisation options
Hole size and throat matched to your fixture, printed information and barcode position, board count and display layout; exact specs based on measured in-store fixtures.
Related products

SCENE 03

Apparel & Accessories Packaging

Apparel, socks and small accessories need packaging that protects the item while presenting a clean brand label. Paper hang tags and hooks concentrate product information on one tidy display face, suiting rail displays and gift-box pairing, and giving clothing and accessories a consistent, minimal look on shelf.

Problem it solves：Solves cluttered accessory displays and weak brand-label visibility
Related product types
General paper hang-card hook (H04), multi-purpose paper hook (H07)
How it is used
Combine the paper hook with a hang tag or box, thread through the garment or accessory and hang on a display rail; smaller accessories can use a smaller hook for better proportion.
Suitable customers
Apparel brands, garment manufacturers, accessory suppliers, gift packaging buyers
Customisation options
Hook shape and size, tag shape, board material and weight, printing and foil finishing; load-related figures to be confirmed by testing.
Related products

SCENE 04

Brand Custom Packaging

Packaging is the first point where a brand is seen on shelf. Paper hooks and hang tags can be customised in shape, colour, print and finishing to your brand identity — turning packaging from a simple fastener into part of brand recognition, especially for customers who want to upgrade store visuals without changing their display systems.

Problem it solves：Solves low brand recognition and inconsistent in-store visuals
Related product types
Kraft question-mark hooks (H06, H08) and custom hang tags
How it is used
Print brand colours and logos on hooks and tags, add spot colour, CMYK, foil or emboss finishing, and display uniformly across your store fixtures for consistent identity.
Suitable customers
Brand owners, packaging design agencies, e-commerce brands, chain-store visual buyers
Customisation options
Logo and artwork printing, board material and colour, spot/CMYK printing, foil, emboss and deboss finishing; feasibility confirmed by samples.

SCENE 05

Wholesale & Retail Packaging

For wholesalers and export buyers, reliable supply, standardisation and controllable cost of packaging components matter just as much. Paper hooks and display boards can be produced in volume to a consistent specification, serving both export packing and retail shelving — helping customers simplify their sourcing range and reduce communication and inventory costs.

Problem it solves：Solves fragmented component sourcing and awkward wholesale-to-retire handover
Related product types
Long/short paper sock boards (B05), multi-scene boards (B06), standard hooks
How it is used
Produce to a unified specification and print for volume orders; goods are supplied in cartons or board packs and go straight to retail shelving or repacking on arrival.
Suitable customers
Wholesalers, export buyers, packaging distributors, large retail groups
Customisation options
Style mix, specifications and print, pack quantity and carton layout; MOQ, lead time and pricing confirmed per inquiry.
