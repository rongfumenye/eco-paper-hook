How to Design Custom Sock Display Cards

By KALOZEN · Specifying sock display cards and header cards for retail

Sock display cards (also called sock header cards or display boards) carry and present socks on a shelf, peg or display rail. A good design starts with how many pairs the card holds and how it will be displayed — then works back to structure and print.

1. Choose the card structure

  • Single-pair header card: a printed card fixed to one packed pair, often with a hang hole.
  • Multi-pair display board: a board that presents several pairs together for in-store or trade-show display.
  • Folded or insert card: a card that folds around or slots into the pack, depending on how the socks are presented.

KALOZEN's B01–B06 range covers display boards and cards in different directions; each is a customisable option rather than a confirmed stock SKU.

2. Confirm hanging and print requirements

  • Hang hole: round hole, airplane slot or no hole — matched to the display fixture.
  • Card size and thickness: set by the number of pairs and the board grade.
  • Print sides: single- or double-sided, with brand and size information positioned for visibility.
  • Fold style: flat, folded or insert, depending on the pack construction.
Card size, thickness, hole type, fold style, pairs per card and print sides are Custom Specifications, confirmed by drawing and approved sample. No fixed dimensions or load values are assumed.

3. Artwork and sampling workflow

Provide the intended display setting, pairs per card, rough size and artwork intent. KALOZEN coordinates dieline and layout review, sample approval and production through manufacturing partners. Zhengzhou is the business coordination location, not a self-owned factory.

4. Difference from paper hooks

A paper hook attaches a pack to a rail; a display card presents the product itself on a printed board. Both are part of Retail Display Packaging and can be specified by structure, size and printing — see Paper Hooks vs Plastic Hooks for the hook side of the comparison.
