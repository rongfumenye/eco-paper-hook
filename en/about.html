Manufacturing Network & Quality Control

KALOZEN produces through reliable partner factories and a coordinated supply chain. We coordinate raw materials, die-cutting, printing and finishing from Zhengzhou, and handle order management, quality inspection and delivery arrangements — we do not claim to own all facilities or production lines.

Our role

Managing orders, safeguarding quality

What determines lead time and consistency is stable partnerships, ready-made tooling for common specs and default eco-friendly materials. We handle requirement confirmation, production coordination, follow-up and pre-shipment inspection; actual converting is carried out by partner paper-product factories.

  • Raw board from stable, traceable suppliers
  • Ready tooling for common sizes; fast setup for custom sizes
  • Die-cutting, printing and foiling executed by partners to spec
  • Pre-shipment inspection of board, thickness, colour and quantity

Capacity

Subject to order confirmation

Monthly capacity, equipment lists and specific line data will be provided only after verification. We do not publish unverified production figures here; share your target quantity and lead time and we will propose a feasible plan based on actual supply-chain capacity.

Production confirmation

Live production confirmation available

To understand the actual status of your order, you can request a live production confirmation: we arrange a real-time link with the partner factory during converting and packing, showing board intake, die-cutting/printing and carton packing. This does not replace a formal audit but helps you see real progress.
