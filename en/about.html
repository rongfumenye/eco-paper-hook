Manufacturing Network & Quality Control

Zhengzhou Coordination Office

Zhengzhou Coordination Office

KALOZEN's Zhengzhou Coordination Office manages customer communication, product specification confirmation, material and process coordination, sample approval, production follow-up, quality-check coordination and export delivery arrangements. Actual material supply and manufacturing are carried out by our supply-chain and manufacturing partners.

Our role

Managing orders, safeguarding quality

Lead time and consistency depend on clear responsibilities and reliable coordination across the supply chain. KALOZEN manages requirements, specifications, materials, artwork, samples, production follow-up and pre-shipment checks; actual manufacturing is carried out by our partner factories.

MANUFACTURING PARTNERS RESPONSIBLE

Paperboard is sourced through supply-chain partners, with material details confirmed for each order. Tooling availability and custom-size processing arrangements are confirmed according to product specifications and partner capabilities.

Workflow

Five steps from material to delivery

Actual die-cutting, printing, hot stamping, embossing and forming are carried out by our partner factories. KALOZEN coordinates specifications, artwork, progress checks and delivery.

01 Material Sourcing

Material Sourcing

Paperboard is sourced through supply-chain partners. Material specifications and supporting documents are confirmed according to the actual materials and documentation available from suppliers.

02 Partner Manufacturing

Partner Manufacturing

Partner factories die-cut and form to the confirmed specification; KALOZEN follows up on progress. All converting is performed by the partner factory, not by KALOZEN.

03 Printing & Finishing

Printing & Finishing

Printing, hot stamping (foiling), embossing and other finishing are executed by partner factories to the approved artwork; KALOZEN coordinates the process and artwork confirmation.

04 Quality Inspection

Quality Inspection

Pre-shipment checks are coordinated by KALOZEN against the confirmed order specification: dimensions, hole diameter, thickness, colour, quantity and packing.

05 Packaging & Delivery

Packaging & Delivery

Cartons are packed and marked to export requirements by partners; KALOZEN coordinates delivery arrangements.

Capacity

Subject to order confirmation

We do not publish unverified monthly capacity figures, equipment lists or production-line data. After you provide your target quantity and lead time, KALOZEN assesses feasibility based on the actual situation of our partner supply chain; any arrangement is confirmed in the order documents.

Production confirmation

Production Updates Upon Request

Depending on partner availability and order status, KALOZEN may arrange production photos or video updates during manufacturing and packing. These updates help customers follow order progress but do not replace a formal factory audit or independent inspection.
