FACTORY

In Zhengzhou,
where every hook gets made right.

Our company is based in Zhengzhou, Henan, working with an established paper-converting supply chain for die-cutting, printing and finishing. What really drives lead time and cost is not the office address, but steady production lines, ready-made cutting dies and default eco materials — which is why a 10,000-piece order can ship in a week without a tooling surcharge.

WHY LEAD TIME IS STABLE

Steady lines, predictable lead time

Board, dies, printing and finishing are coordinated across long-standing production partners. Common die profiles are kept on hand, and custom ones are arranged quickly. That is the difference between a 7-day and a 25-day lead time.

  • Board and greyboard from reliable suppliers
  • In-house die cutting, printing and foil stamping
  • Shipping via Qingdao and Shanghai ports
  • Consolidation with tags and cards available

MILESTONES

How we got here

2016

Started with hang tags

Began as a hang tag and card converter for domestic sock factories in Datang.

2019

First paper hook die line

Developed the first S-hook profile in response to a European buyer asking for a plastic-free alternative.

2021

FSC® chain of custody

Certified the converting process so hooks could ship with documentation for EU retail compliance.

2023

Export share passes 40%

Europe and North America become the majority of volume as single-use plastic rules tightened.

2025

Twelve standard profiles

Expanded from socks into underwear, home textile, pet, baby and beauty categories.

OUR GUARANTEES

Three promises we put in the contract

  • Sample matches production. If the delivered goods differ from the approved sample in board, thickness or colour, we remake the batch.
  • Lead time or notice. If a confirmed delivery date slips, you are told before the date, not after.
  • Quantity counted twice. Short shipments are topped up at our cost on the next dispatch.
