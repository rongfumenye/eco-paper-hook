SURFACE OPTIONS

Four ways to look

The same hook reads very differently depending on board and finish.

Natural kraft

Unbleached, visible fibre. The default eco signal — reads as recycled without a word of copy.

Bleached white

Clean and clinical. Preferred for beauty, baby and pharmacy categories.

Matte black

Premium apparel and gifting. Hides print imperfections; needs a thicker board to hold shape.

Foil stamped

Gold or silver logo on kraft or black. The cheapest way to lift the perceived value of the whole pack.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask first

Will a paper hook hold a heavy multi-pack?

Up to roughly 600 g with a 2.0 mm reinforced profile in dry conditions. Above that we recommend a double hook to split the load across two points, or a plastic hook if the weight is unavoidable.

Does the hole fit standard pegboard?

Standard is 5 mm, which suits most retail pegboard and 4–6 mm display rods. Send us the peg diameter and we drill to match — a 1 mm difference is the most common reason a hook fails on site.

What happens in humid storage?

Paper absorbs moisture and loses stiffness. For unairconditioned warehouses in tropical markets we recommend a moisture-barrier liner and a 2.0 mm board, or a coated grade.

Can you print our logo and barcode?

Yes. Up to four spot colours, plus barcode and QR. Artwork should be vector; we return a digital proof before tooling.

What is the minimum order?

10,000 pieces for standard profiles, 30,000 for custom die-cut. Sampling is available before you commit to production.
