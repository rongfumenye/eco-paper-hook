FAQ

Five things buyers ask first

Can paper hooks hold heavier multi-packs?

In dry conditions the 2.0 mm heavy-duty hook holds up to ~600 g. Beyond that, split the load across two points; if weight cannot be reduced, a plastic hook is the right call.

Will the hole fit a standard pegboard?

Standard is 5 mm, fitting most retail pegboards and 4–6 mm bars. Tell us your bar diameter and we drill to size — a 1 mm mismatch is the usual cause of a poor fit.

What happens in humid storage?

Paper absorbs moisture and softens. For tropical markets without air-conditioned warehousing, add a moisture liner and use 2.0 mm board.

Can you print our logo and barcode?

Yes. Up to four spot colours, plus barcode and QR code. Provide vector artwork; we send a digital proof before cutting the die.

What is the minimum order?

10,000 pcs for standard profiles, 30,000 pcs for custom die-cut. Samples are available before production.
