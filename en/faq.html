FAQ

Are samples free?

In-stock samples of standard hook types are free; you cover shipping. Custom samples are quoted per item; tooling cost can be credited against mass-production orders.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ is confirmed by style and printing. Please state your requirements in the inquiry and we will propose an option.

What is the lead time?

Timing depends on quantity and finishing and is confirmed in writing.

Which certificates can you provide?

FSC® chain-of-custody documents, material declarations, and ink/varnish food-contact compliance documents; scope per certificate.

Can you ship to Amazon FBA?

Yes. We can label and mark cartons per FBA requirements and provide packing photos before shipment.

Do you work with trading companies?

Yes. We supply both direct buyers and traders and will not contact your end customers.

How do I inquire?

Fill the inquiry form or WhatsApp with product type, quantity, material and specs.
