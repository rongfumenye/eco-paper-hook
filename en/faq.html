FAQ

What is KALOZEN?

KALOZEN is a packaging solutions partner for global buyers, covering two directions: Retail Display Packaging (eco paper hooks, sock display cards and display boards) and Food & Beverage Packaging (paper bags, boxes, bowls, cups, trays, straws, napkins and wooden cutlery).

Does KALOZEN own a manufacturing factory?

No. KALOZEN works on a partner supply chain model and coordinates production through manufacturing partners. Zhengzhou is our business coordination location, not a self-owned factory.

What is the difference between Eco Paper Hooks and Sock Display Cards?

Eco paper hooks are die-cut paper hooks that attach sock packaging to a display rail; sock display cards are printed paper boards that carry and present one or more pairs of socks. Both belong to Retail Display Packaging and are customised by structure, size and printing.

How do I request customised specifications?

Send an inquiry with your product direction, intended use, quantity, target size or structure, and printing requirements. Specifications are confirmed by drawing and sample; unconfirmed values are marked Custom Specifications or To be confirmed.

Are samples free?

Sample availability, cost and shipping are confirmed by item in your inquiry. Custom samples are quoted per item; any tooling cost is confirmed in writing.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ is confirmed by style and printing. Please state your requirements in the inquiry and we will propose an option.

What is the lead time?

Timing depends on quantity and finishing and is confirmed in writing.

Which certificates can you provide?

Where required by your order, we coordinate documents actually held by the partner manufacturer, such as chain-of-custody, material declarations or food-contact compliance papers. Scope is subject to the valid certificate; we do not claim unverified certification.

Can you ship to Amazon FBA?

Yes. We can label and mark cartons per FBA requirements and provide packing photos before shipment.

Do you work with trading companies?

Yes. We supply both direct buyers and traders and will not contact your end customers.

How do I inquire?

Fill the inquiry form or WhatsApp with product type, quantity, material and specs.
