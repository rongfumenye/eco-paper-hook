FAQ

Before you write

Do you send free samples?

Stock samples of standard profiles can be sent free of charge; you cover courier. Custom samples are quoted per design and the tooling cost can be credited against the production order.

Can you ship to Amazon FBA?

Yes. We can pack to FBA requirements with your labels and carton markings, and send carton photos before dispatch.

What certificates can you provide?

FSC® chain-of-custody documentation, material declarations and, where relevant, food-contact compliance for the inks and varnish used.

Do you work with trading companies?

Yes. We supply both direct buyers and trading partners, and we do not contact your customers.
