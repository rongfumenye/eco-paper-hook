THREE PILLARS

Sustainability you can audit, not just advertise

Every claim on this page traces to a document, a certificate or a test. We do not sell sentiment — we sell facts your buyer can file.

Traceable fibre

FSC®-certified kraft board and greyboard from responsibly managed forests. Chain-of-custody certificate number supplied with every shipment, ready for your retail compliance file.

Plastic-free construction

Die-cut from a single sheet. No film, no lamination, no adhesive, no metal insert. The hook recycles in the same stream as the hang tag — no disassembly.

Recyclable & compostable

Accepted by standard waste-paper recycling. Degrades under industrial composting. Print “paper hook — recycle with card” and the loop closes at the consumer.
