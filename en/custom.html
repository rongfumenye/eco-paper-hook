FOUR-STEP PROCESS

From Brief to Delivery in Four Steps

The flow below describes how we coordinate the work; sampling and production arrangements are confirmed by the actual order.

STEP 1

Brief & artwork

Provide product type, dimensions, material and printing intent, estimated quantity and target market. If you do not have artwork, product photos, dimensions or reference images are also welcome — KALOZEN will help coordinate the design proposal.

STEP 2

Digital proof

A digital proof is prepared to confirm content, position and colour direction. The format and timing of the proof are assessed against the specific proposal and confirmed in practice.

STEP 3

Physical sample approval

A physical sample is produced. We recommend testing it on a real shelf or in the actual packaging setting before proceeding. Sample arrangements and timing are confirmed by the actual order.

STEP 4

Production coordination & delivery

After sample approval, KALOZEN coordinates order production with its manufacturing partners. Production progress, quality checks, packaging and delivery arrangements are managed according to the confirmed order requirements. Payment terms are subject to the final agreement between both parties.
