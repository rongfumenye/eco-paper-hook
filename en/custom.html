CUSTOM & OEM

Your shape,
your print, our tooling.

Most customers start from a standard profile and change the print. Some need a silhouette that matches a brand mark. Both are fine — the difference is tooling and lead time, not capability.

FIVE DIMENSIONS

What can be customised

Change one dimension or all five. Each has a different cost and lead-time implication.

DIMENSION 01

Shape

Start from an existing SKU or supply a die line. Custom silhouettes need a cutting die; timing to be confirmed.

DIMENSION 02

Board & colour

Kraft, bleached white, matte black, or a Pantone-matched dyed board for larger runs.

DIMENSION 03

Printing

Up to four spot colours, soy-based ink. Logo, size marker, barcode, QR, recycling instruction.

DIMENSION 04

Finishing

Hot foil in gold, silver or matte black; emboss and deboss for tactile branding.

DIMENSION 05

Dimensions

Hole diameter, throat width, overall length. This is where fit is won or lost — measure the peg, not the drawing.

SAMPLING PROCESS

Four steps from artwork to container

Sampling and production times vary with style, finishing and quantity. The flow below is indicative; exact timings to be confirmed.

STEP 1

Brief & artwork

You send artwork (AI, PDF or EPS), target quantity and market. We confirm feasibility and quote tooling and lead time.

STEP 2

Digital proof

We return a print proof showing colour, position and die line. Nothing is cut until this is signed off.

STEP 3

Physical sample

Cut and printed sample shipped by courier, timing to be confirmed. Test it on your actual fixture before approving.

STEP 4

Production

after sample approval; timing confirmed in writing. Carton photos sent before dispatch; balance settled against shipping documents.

ARTWORK SPECS

Files we can work from

  • Vector preferred: AI, EPS or PDF with outlined text
  • Die line: separate layer, 100% scale, no bleed on the cut path
  • Bleed: 2 mm minimum on printed edges
  • Colour: Pantone references, not RGB screenshots
  • Barcode: vector, with quiet zone respected

Send low-resolution files for quoting. We only need production files after the sample is approved.

HONEST LIMITS

What we will tell you not to do

Three requests we push back on, because they fail in the field and the complaint lands on us.

Very thin board, heavy product

1.5 mm under a 700 g multi-pack will bow within days. Use 2.0 mm or split the load with a heavy-duty hook.

Paper hook for wet environments

Chilled or humid displays cause swelling and loss of stiffness. A coated grade helps, but plastic is still the right answer there.

Full-colour photo print

Uncoated kraft mutes photographic print badly. For photo-real artwork, use a coated tag and keep the hook to a spot colour.
